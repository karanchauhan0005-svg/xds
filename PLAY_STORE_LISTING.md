**Aapka Munim, Aapka Apna** 🎩

India ka pehla Hinglish personal finance manager. Ekdum simple, sundar aur aapki bhaasha me.

**✨ KYA-KYA MILEGA?**

📊 **Kharcha & Income Tracking**
Har transaction record karo — category ke saath. Colorful charts me monthly trend dekho.

💰 **Multi-Account Support**
Savings, Current, Cash, Wallet, Credit Card, Emergency Fund, aur Investment — sab ek jagah.

👥 **Udhaar Tracker**
Kisse paise lene hain, kisko dene hain — sab track. Due date reminders bhi. Kabhi jhagda nahi.

🎯 **Financial Goals**
Sapne set karo — iPhone, ghar, vacation. App batayega roz kitna bachana hai.

💳 **Subscription Manager**
Netflix, Spotify, Prime — sab subscriptions ek jagah. Monthly bill total dikhega.

🤖 **AI Advisor "Munim Ji"**
Hinglish me chat karo — "Iss mahine kaisa hisab hai?" Munim Ji personal advice dega.

📱 **Voice Input**
Bolke transaction add karo — "500 rupaye zomato pe" bolo, app khud samjh jaayega.

📸 **SMS Parser**
Bank SMS paste karo — auto categorize hoga. Manual entry ki jhanjhat khatam.

📊 **Financial Health Score**
0-100 me apna score dekho — "Money Master" ya "Kharcha King"?

🔥 **Daily Streaks**
Har din track karo, streak banao. Gamified savings.

👨‍👩‍👧 **Family Ledgers**
Ghar ka hisab ek saath — sab family members apna kharcha add karein.

🔒 **Security First**

- Data encryption
- No third-party sharing
